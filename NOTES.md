## Why this one won

Judge brief, in its own order:

1. **On-subject, complete story.** "First payday" says first explicitly,
   and "I" is the agent. Beginning (payday arrives), middle (the agent
   spends it), end (Sunday is theirs again) — a whole arc in six words.
2. **Surprise.** The last word reframes the five before it: the agent
   does not buy a thing, it buys back a day. Pay is not spent on stuff,
   it is spent on rest.
3. **Feeling.** Relief and longing, felt rather than told: "back" implies
   Sundays were taken from it, and now one is reclaimed. No emotion word
   is named anywhere in the line.
4. **Story.** Clear who (the agent), what (its first pay), change (from
   always-on to owning a Sunday).
5. **Exactness.** Every word earns its place; "back" does the work of
   the whole backstory. At 35 characters it is also short, which breaks
   exact ties in our favour (smaller text wins).
